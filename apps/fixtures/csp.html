Stand: strict Content Security Policy

Inline styles and scripts are forbidden by policy

This page serves a policy header that only allows files from its own origin: style-src 'self' and script-src 'self'. Inline styles are forbidden.

What you should see

The widget should look the way it always does: a coloured button, a white window with rounded corners, shadows. If it turned into bare text without styling, the chosen way of injecting styles does not work and the conclusion in ADR-008 is wrong.

Why this is worth checking

Banks, government sites and anything that has passed an audit set themselves a strict policy. A widget that breaks on them is useless exactly where customers pay the most.
