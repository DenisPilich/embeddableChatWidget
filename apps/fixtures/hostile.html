Stand: hostile layout

Checking that the widget survives someone else's page

Everything here is done "however it turned out": Comic Sans with !important, triple line spacing, stretched letters, scrolling disabled and a foreign layer with a gigantic z-index.

What you should see

The widget button sits in the bottom right corner, above the reddish foreign layer. The text inside the widget keeps a plain sans-serif font, normal line spacing and normal casing. If it started to look like this page, the isolation is not working.

Why this matters

The widget gets embedded into pages written by other people, pages we are not responsible for. We cannot ask them to drop !important or fix their z-index — the only way out is for our code not to depend on their markup.
