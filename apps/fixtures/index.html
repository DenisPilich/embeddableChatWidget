Try the chat widget

  1. Ask the assistant something in the corner — for example, about opening hours.
  2. Press Talk to a human in the chat window when you want an operator instead of the assistant.
  3. Open the operator dashboard and sign in as demo@example.com / demo-operator-2026. The conversation will be waiting in the queue, and an answer written there appears in the widget.

Answers come from a real model, and the free plan allows about twenty questions a day — worth knowing before asking a hundred. The chat window remembers the conversation in the browser, so it survives a page reload.
